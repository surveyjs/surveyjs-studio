# User guide

How to use My Surveys. The guide grows with the application.

- [Your surveys](your-surveys.md): the survey list, projects, Active and Archived, creating a survey and loading more.
- [Projects and team members](projects-and-team-members.md): the Projects page, creating, renaming and deleting a project, adding members, admins, leaving, and what members can do with project surveys.
- [Managing a survey](managing-a-survey.md): rename, the "…" menu, clone, publish, copy IDs, move, archive, restore and delete.
- [Designing a survey](designing-a-survey.md): the editor, autosave, conflicts, Clear Survey, the obfuscated export, themes, embed code and read-only mode.
- [Images and files in your survey](images-and-files.md): uploading pictures and videos, the allowed types, the limit and what happens to files on clone and delete.
- [Publishing and collecting responses](publishing-and-collecting-responses.md): publish and unpublish, the run link, what respondents see, "Limit to one response", file questions, "Save Respondent IP", pre-filled answers and the response count.
- [Viewing results: Summary and Table](viewing-results.md): the results page, charts, the table, exports, saved layouts and their reset.
- [Downloading, sharing and deleting results](downloading-sharing-and-deleting-results.md): the Details tab: totals and dates, the JSON and CSV downloads, Grant Access and the public results page, private questions, "Save Respondent IP" and Delete Results.
- [Survey history](survey-history.md): the list of saved versions of a survey.
- [Moving from the old My Surveys](moving-from-the-old-my-surveys.md): where things moved, what stays the same, and what deleting your surveyjs.io account does.
- [Signing in](signing-in.md): sign in, the account menu, sign out, licence notices and the cookie banner.
